# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, summarised here.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind to other people and respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use MasterSafe and for the community

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Disclosing a security problem publicly before it is fixed, instead of reporting it privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities and scope

The project maintainer clarifies and enforces these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that do not follow them, explaining the reason when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews), and when someone
officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@GabrielVergara](https://github.com/GabrielVergara), using the
contact details on their GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the
person reporting are respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning that explains why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
